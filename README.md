# Research Project: Community-Led Growth for B2B SaaS

## Topic
Community-led Growth- how B2B SaaS companies build and monetize communities to drive growth

## Why I chose this topic
Community growth is important and sits at the intersection of social media and business strategy.

## Experts I researched
10 Practioners who actively build communities and/or run community-led companies.
Full list - [research/sources.md](research/sources.md)

## Repository structure
`/research/sources.md` — all experts with links and annotations
`/research/linkedin-posts/` — posts organized by author
 `/research/youtube-transcripts/` — transcripts organized by author
`/research/other/` — podcast notes, articles, etc.

## How content was collected
YouTube transcripts via Python script using `youtube-transcript-api`
LinkedIn posts manually collected from each expert's profile
Scripts were written with help of Claude AI
