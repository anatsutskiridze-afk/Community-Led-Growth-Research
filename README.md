# Research Project: Community-Led Growth for B2B SaaS

## Topic
Community-Led Growth (CLG) — how B2B SaaS companies build, nurture, and
monetize communities to drive acquisition, retention, and expansion.

## Why I chose this topic
Community-led growth sits at the intersection of social media, human
psychology, and business strategy. Unlike paid ads or cold outreach, CLG
creates compounding value — a strong community sells your product for you.
As SaaS markets get more competitive, this is becoming a core GTM motion.

## Why I chose these experts
I specifically avoided the most obvious Google results and looked for
practitioners who have actually built communities, not just written about them.
Sources found via CMX Hub, Bevy blog, LinkedIn post search, and Community
Signal podcast.
