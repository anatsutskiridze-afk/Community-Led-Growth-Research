"Where Stars Are Born": How We Built iSpring's Global L&D Community on LinkedIn to 11,000+ Professionals in Under a Year

We grew a global L&D community on LinkedIn from around 1,000 to 11,000+ professionals across 30+ countries in just 11 months — and turned it into a real engine for brand awareness, trust-building, audience warm-up, and lead generation, across the U.S., Canada, and Europe.

One of the biggest lessons from this case: in complex B2B, community is no longer a side initiative. When built strategically, it can become one of the strongest growth, trust, and commercial assets a company has.
