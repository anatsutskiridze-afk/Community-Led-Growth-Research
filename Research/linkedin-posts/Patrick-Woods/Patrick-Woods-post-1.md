When talking to leaders who don't inherently "get" community, we're increasingly not saying the C Word (i.e. "community") at all, but rather focusing on the powerful outcomes that emerge from communities to impact the business in ways execs understand.

There are many such outcomes, but product feedback, UGC, and ambassador-style brand amplification can have a huge impact when systematized and scaled effectively.

And by the way, one big outcome of such programs turns out to be...community!
