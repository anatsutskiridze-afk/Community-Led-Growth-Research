Friction in product onboarding can be good, actually - when it helps your 
customer more quickly reach the value they're seeking.

"Friction is [only] a problem when it doesn't serve your customer. When it 
is only there to feed the curiosity of the business, or move you to the right 
sales rep, or carry a burden the product team hasn't solved yet. That kind of 
friction is a zero value-add for your potential customer."

---
*Why saved:* Insight on customer-led thinking — distinguishing friction that serves the customer vs. the business. Core principle for building communities 
around real user needs rather than company goals.
